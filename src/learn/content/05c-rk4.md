# RK4

## Idea

Sample the slope four times across the step (at the start, twice in the middle, and at the end) and take a weighted average.

## Analogy

Tasting the soup four times before deciding how much salt to add.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Fourth order, so very accurate per step. It is not symplectic: the energy error drifts, roughly in proportion to time. For a short run it is excellent; for ten thousand orbits it is not.

## Cost

Four force evaluations per step. To compare it fairly, give it four times leapfrog's step.

## When to use it

Short, accurate runs, and as the baseline everything else is compared to.

:::deeper
$\mathbf k_1=f(y)$, $\mathbf k_2=f(y+\tfrac{dt}{2}\mathbf k_1)$, $\mathbf k_3=f(y+\tfrac{dt}{2}\mathbf k_2)$, $\mathbf k_4=f(y+dt\,\mathbf k_3)$, then $y\leftarrow y+\tfrac{dt}{6}(\mathbf k_1+2\mathbf k_2+2\mathbf k_3+\mathbf k_4)$, with $y=(\mathbf x,\mathbf v)$ and $f=(\mathbf v,\mathbf a)$.
:::
